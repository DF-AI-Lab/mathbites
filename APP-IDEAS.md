# MathBites: App Ideas 💡

Ideas collected while studying for Functional Skills Maths Level 2.
To grill and plan later.

---

## 1. Better working-out space

- **Full-page jotter**: it opens to fill the whole screen, not a small box
- Keep all 3 modes: **Jotter**, **Squares**, **Scribble**
- **Save the working out** with each question
- **Photo of the question** saved next to the working out
- Use this for data: look back at where mistakes happened

---

## 2. Halving helper (fractions)

- Halving big numbers is where mistakes creep in
  - e.g. 280 → 130 ❌ (should be 140)
- Idea: a "chunk it" hint, e.g. 280 = 200 + 80 → 100 + 40 = 140
- Simplifying steps to show: halve → try 3, 5, 7 → stop

---

## 3. Reverse check (double-check answers)

- After answering, the app helps you work it **backwards**
  - e.g. 2/5 → times both by 12 → 24/60 ✅ matches the question
- **Sense check** prompt: "Is the top less than half the bottom? Then the answer is under 1/2"
- **Copy check**: "Did you copy the numbers right?" (e.g. 60 written as 30)

---

## 4. "Before you check" list

A quick pop-up before pressing Check:

1. Did I copy the numbers right?
2. Radius or diameter?
3. Triangle or semicircle? **Halve it!**

---

## 5. Circles & shapes picture cards

One simple picture per idea, same colours every time:

- 🔴 **Radius**: centre → edge (r = d ÷ 2)
- 🔵 **Diameter**: all the way across (d = r × 2)
- 🟡 **Circumference** = the belt 👖: π × **d** (answer in m)
- 🟢 **Area** = the pizza inside 🍕: π × **r** × **r** (answer in m²)
- 🌗 **Semicircle**
  - Area: halve it
  - Edge: halve the circumference, **then add the flat edge**
- 🍊 **Surface area** = the peel / whole outside of a 3D shape
  - Cylinder (tin): 2πr² + 2πrh (lid + base + label)
  - Sphere (football ⚽): 4 × π × r × r (= 4 flat circles)
- 🔺 **Triangle** = box ÷ 2 (halve the base, the height, or the answer)

Memory hooks:

- **C**ircumference → **D**iameter (C and D are next to each other)
- **A**rea → "**r r**" like a growl 🐯
- See **²** in the answer box? It's an area

## 6. Mistake tracker

- Log the *type* of slip, not just wrong/right
  - e.g. forgot to halve, used diameter not radius, copied wrong, halving error
- Show patterns: "You forget to halve 3 times out of 5"
- Based on real result: Measure **E3.8**, 83% to Level 1, almost all marks lost to slips

---

## 7. "Write the line, π last" helper

- Make you **write the whole line first** before working it out
  - e.g. 3 × 2 × 2 × 5
- **Numbers first, π last** 🥧
  - r × r → × height → × π at the end
  - Stops π getting "in the way" and being used twice (3 × 2 × 3 ❌)
- Hook: **one π, two r's** 🐯
- Works the same when π = 3.14: only the last step changes

---

## 8. Units cards: length, area, volume

- 📏 **Length** = plain (m): circumference, perimeter
- 🟩 **Area** = ² (m²): flat, shaded space
- 🧊 **Volume** = ³ (m³): filling a 3D shape
- Trick: **count the lengths multiplied** → 1 = m, 2 = m², 3 = m³
- Pick the unit **after** working out which one it is
- 🍊 Peel of a ball = surface area (m²). 🫗 Filling it = volume (m³)
- **Cylinder volume** = stack of pizzas: πr² × h
- **0.4² grid card**: 1 m × 1 m square split 10 × 10
  - Shade 4 × 4 = 16 out of 100 = 0.16 m²
  - Under 1, squared → smaller. Over 1, squared → bigger
- Conversions card
  - kilo = 1,000 · milli = 1,000 small bits · centi = 100
  - **1 cm³ = 1 ml**, 1,000 cm³ = 1 litre

## 9. R.W.S. slip fix + "guess first"

Before every answer:

1. **Read it twice** 👀 (point at the numbers)
2. **Write the whole line** ✍️
3. **Sense check** 🤔: does this size make sense?
   - 5 feet = about a person's height → 150 cm ✅, not 2.5 cm ❌

App idea: a **"guess first" box**
- Make a rough guess **before** working it out
- Then compare: way off? Check again

---

## 10. Box surface area: face-by-face

- **3 pairs of faces, each × 2**: front/back, ends, top/bottom
- Show a table and tick each pair off ✔️✔️✔️
- Each face is a different size, so check which two sides it uses
- Based on the snake tank (2 × 1 × 0.5 = 7 m²): missed the top and bottom

---

## 11. Quick-fire practice rounds

- 5 short questions, easy numbers (π = 3), no calculator
- Talk it through while writing on Scribble
- Mix in traps: diameter given for area, semicircles, units
- Score each round and show progress
  - Real rounds: 3/5 → 2/5 → **5/5** once working was written down

---

## ✅ Proof it works

- Before: Measure **Entry 3.8**
- After one session of circles, units and writing it down: Measure **Level 2.0** 🚀 (22/11/2026, about 11 mins)
- Only about 2 wrong, both slips (copy slip + missed faces)
