# Math Bites

Bite-size maths practice for **Functional Skills Maths Level 2**. Do 3–15 minutes a day, with a streak and XP.

## What's in it

- **Give me 5**: mixed questions, one mistake to spot and fix, and one formula card
- **Spot the mistake**: find the wrong line in someone's working, then type the right answer
- **Squares = column maths**: numbers line up on the right, = draws a double answer line, answer fills right to left, Carry adds small slanted carry digits in any square you tap; digits in the row under the answer become small carries and the box jumps back up
- **Squares builds itself**: + − × ÷ starts the next row with the sign next to the number, = draws a line that only covers the columns used, and a "Use answer" button on the left puts the answer in the box (or picks the matching option)
- **Short pick 'em answers** show 2 × 2
- **Formula cards**: pick the right rule from 5. The wrong ones are real rules from other cards
- **Type it in**: some questions have no options, like the real exam
- **Working out**: type-in questions have scrap paper. Scribble with your finger, use a handwritten jotter, or use squared paper. Calculator questions add a Calc tab
- **2 goes**: get it wrong and you get one more go before the answer shows
- **Your name** is saved on this phone only
- **Progress**: score today, day streak, right-in-a-row streak, % right this week and month (💯 at 100%)
- **Weak topics**: topics under 70% over your last 10 tries, with a "Practise weak topics" round
- **App icon** top left on home; on the question screen it takes you home (asks first if you are mid-round)
- **Settings (⋮)**: change name, text size (Auto fits your screen), reset progress, version number
- **Answer bar**: answer box and Check on one line, and tabs go Jotter, Squares, Scribble, Calc
- **Spot the mistake**: after you check, every line says why it is right or wrong
- **Squares = column maths**: numbers line up on the right, = draws a double answer line, answer fills right to left, Carry adds small slanted carry digits in any square you tap; digits in the row under the answer become small carries and the box jumps back up
- **Squares builds itself**: + − × ÷ starts the next row with the sign next to the number, = draws a line that only covers the columns used, and a "Use answer" button on the left puts the answer in the box (or picks the matching option)
- **Short pick 'em answers** show 2 × 2
- **Scrap paper** on home: Jotter, Squares, Scribble and Calc on a full-screen page for any working out. Your working is kept until you clear it
- **Scribble scroll strip**: drag the strip on the right to move up and down the paper without leaving Pen (mouse wheel works too)
- **Faster taps**: keypad buttons register the moment you touch them, so quick taps on a phone are never lost
- **PC keyboard**: number keys and + − × ÷ = type into Jotter, Squares and Calc (Enter = new line, Backspace = delete, C = carry in Squares)
- **Version** shows under the logo (v1.13.0)
- **Get it wrong → see the formula**: the formula card for that topic pops up
- **Calculator mode**: a compact on-screen calculator with π, x², √, xʸ and (−) for minus numbers. Its "Put in answer" button drops the result into the box
- Every question is random each time, with friendly numbers

## Topics

Number, measures/shape/space, and handling data, as covered by the Level 2 spec.

## Run it

It's one file: open `index.html` in a browser.

To use it on your phone, turn on GitHub Pages:
**Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root**.

Progress is saved on your device only.
